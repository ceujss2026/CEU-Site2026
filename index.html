CAREER EDUCATION UNIT
DEPARTMENT OF SCHOOLS
Roles
The career education unit collaborates with school career teachers to provide exposure to enhance students knowledge about career paths, enabling them to make informed choices regarding subjects or courses to pursue at the school level.
Peranan
Unit Pendidikan Kerjaya bekerjasama dengan guru-guru kerjaya sekolah bagi memberikan pendedahan bagi meningkatkan pengetahuan pelajar mengenai hala tuju kerjaya untuk pelajar-pelajar membuat pilihan yang tepat berhubung dengan mata pelajaran atau kursus yang akan diikuti di peringkat persekolahan.
Mission
providing holistic career guidance and consultation services to shape educated, visionary, and competitive students.
Misi
Menyediakan perkhidmatan bimbingan dan konsultasi kerjaya yang holistik ke arah pembentukan pelajar yang berpendidikan, berwawasan dan berdaya saing.
Vision
Towards excellence and achieving student goals so they are more focused.
Visi
Ke arah kecemerlangan dan pencapaian hala tuju pelajar agar lebih terarah.

General Admission Rules
  • Bahasa Melayu: Minimum Grade C6 in BGCE ‘O’ Level Bahasa Melayu / Pengetahuan Agama Islam / Kesusasteraan Melayu.
  • English Language: PASS GCE ‘O’ Level English Language / IGCSE English (as a second language).
  • Credits Criteria: Minimum 4 credits (excluding Malay Language) in GCE ‘O’ Level subjects OR Grade C in IGCSE subjects.
